Point

From Number Theory to Algebraic Geometry and Logic
Weekly Sessions · 2–2.5 Hours · 64 Meetings (P1–P3 + S1–S61)
Final Edition: every session carries all content, proofs, course-role, and references

Course Description

This course traces a single unifying concept — the point — through all its incarnations: a prime number, a valuation, a prime ideal, a closed or generic point of a scheme, a T-valued point via the functor of points, a place of a number field, a geometric morphism into a topos, a model of a logical theory, and a state of a non-commutative algebra. From Gauss's quadratic reciprocity through Galois theory, valuation theory, scheme theory, Class Field Theory, and topos theory, it culminates in Deligne's theorem that every coherent topos has enough points.

Every main theorem is proved. The course is fully self-contained: all prerequisites (groups, topology, algebraic topology, modules, category theory, complex analysis, homological algebra) are built inside the course, just in time.
